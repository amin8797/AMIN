How to use the demonstration

Use the dropdown to compare two short sentences and two artificial attention heads. Hover over any cell to see its weight. Every row sums to 1.0. Observe how the word "not" can attract attention from "good" in the negative sentence. This is a conceptual model, not trained-model output.

Scientific interpretation

All attention weights are hand-constructed solely for instruction. They are not computed from a fitted Transformer, and attention weights alone are not a valid explanation of model decisions or sentiment causality. The comments are invented teaching sentences.
