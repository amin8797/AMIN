What if the prediction is wrong?

The model does not "know" the right answer automatically. During supervised training, the real target supplies an error signal. Backpropagation calculates derivatives, and an optimizer changes the weights. A new forward pass checks whether the prediction changed.

Important scientific limits

These are small, trainable RNN and LSTM networks with authentic analytical BPTT gradients. Sales are simulated for education, not real business records. A single update need not improve every example or the held-out set, and LSTM is not inherently superior in every problem.
